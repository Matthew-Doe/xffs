## Underlying EIO

Kernel records on October 1 (America/New_York) show:

- 22:18:52.740934: `usb 2-4: USB disconnect, device number 3`.
- 22:18:52.750936: offline WRITE error on `/dev/sdb`, sector 8.
- 22:18:52.750993: buffer I/O error on logical block 1.

Sector 8 (512-byte sectors) is XFFS journal-control block 1. The raw inspection
saved before recovery shows control 1 clean at sequence 5281 and control 2
committed at sequence 5280. The transaction was retiring its journal record,
after flushing data and checkpointed metadata. That accounts for complete new
contents despite the application error. The exact failed backend call is not
logged, but the failed sector and durable controls place the interruption in
journal retirement.

Closing both files then triggered FUSE flush on the faulted filesystem, producing
secondary EIO tracebacks. The milestone harness records those expected close
errors without masking unrelated errors. The newer coverage reports explicitly
separate application-write interruption from the journal phase observed before
recovery. No filesystem correctness fix is indicated by this run.

Physical trials sample uncontrolled failure points. The simulator's complete
write/flush-boundary matrix supplies the systematic COW crash coverage.
